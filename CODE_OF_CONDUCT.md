# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, appearance, disability, nationality, ethnicity, religion, or other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and explain technical decisions with evidence.
- Respect privacy and the sensitive nature of email infrastructure.
- Never publish credentials, recipient data, or private message contents.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sustained disruption, publishing private information without consent, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional community are not permitted.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to Olatilewa Olatoye at `tilewa.olatoyee@gmail.com`. Do not open a public issue containing private information. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when this policy is violated.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents this community. SendLib account, billing, or service concerns belong with SendLib's official support channels.
